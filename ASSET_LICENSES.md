# External Asset Licenses

Deuces Arena keeps externally sourced visual assets in this file so their origin and usage terms
remain auditable. Original project artwork is not listed here.

## Orchard Salon

- **Local file:** `apps/web/public/art/orchard-salon-card-back.jpg`
- **Work:** _Still Life: Flowers and Fruit_ (1850-55)
- **Artist:** Severin Roesen
- **Source:** The Metropolitan Museum of Art, object 11938
- **Object page:** https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/11938
- **Image source:** https://images.metmuseum.org/CRDImages/ad/web-large/DT2133.jpg
- **Rights status:** Public Domain; The Met Open Access
- **Use in Deuces Arena:** Displayed as the Orchard Salon card-back artwork and as a cropped,
  translucent detail on the matching card fronts. The original downloaded image is stored without
  pixel-level modification; responsive cropping and overlays are applied by the app at render time.

The Met marks this object and its image as public domain. Its Open Access policy permits unrestricted
commercial and noncommercial use without permission or fee.
